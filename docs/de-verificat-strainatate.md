# De verificat de un om — fișele din străinătate

Scris pe 2026-10-13, după verificarea celor 320 de fișe de universități din străinătate pe site-urile oficiale.
Verificarea a fost făcută de ajutoare automate care au deschis paginile oficiale; mai jos este ce NU au putut confirma
și ce au schimbat la câmpurile „da/nu”. Notele sunt în engleză, așa cum le-au scris verificatorii.

## Rezultatul pe scurt

- fișe confirmate integral: 153
- fișe cu corecturi: 163 (389 corecturi confirmate pe pagini oficiale)
- reformulări prudente, unde afirmația nu a putut fi nici confirmată, nici infirmată: 41
- fișe verificate doar parțial, pentru că site-ul oficial nu s-a deschis: 4

## Hotărâri de luat de echipă

- **Suedia, „Cămin”:** Uppsala și Stockholm au „nu”, celelalte patru au „da”, deși peste tot studentul stă la coadă la o fundație de locuințe studențești. De ales o singură variantă.
- **Danemarca, Copenhaga:** KU are „Cămin: da”, CBS și ITU au „nu”, pentru aceeași situație.
- **Elveția:** ETH Zürich și EPFL au „cost mic”, deși taxa pentru cei veniți din străinătate a crescut mult; USI Lugano are „mediu”.
- **Polonia:** AGH Cracovia are „cost mic”, dar două programe de informatică costă cât SGH („mediu”).
- **Portugalia:** pentru aceeași bursă națională, Porto are „Burse: da”, iar Lisabona „nu”.
- **King's College London:** bursa de merit nu a putut fi confirmată; „Burse: da” a rămas.
- **Coventry:** nivelul de cost a rămas „mare”, deși o bursă pentru UE aduce taxa la nivelul celei britanice.
- **INSA Lyon:** de confirmat care dintre cele două proceduri ale grupului INSA se aplică unui bacalaureat românesc.
- **Moldova:** niciun site nu spune ce taxă plătește un cetățean român; e nevoie de un telefon la universitate.
- **Princeton și Columbia:** testul SAT/ACT e opțional doar în acest an de admitere; fișele trebuie schimbate când redevine obligatoriu.
- **Capacitatea căminelor:** numerele exacte de locuri au fost înlocuite cu aproximări în cuvinte („câteva sute”, „câteva mii”). Au rămas câteva luni orientative („din iunie”, „în ianuarie”), acolo unde verificatorii le-au confirmat.

## Câmpuri „da/nu” și niveluri de cost schimbate

- **VIA University College (Danemarca)**, `dorms`: false → true. VIA has no dorms of its own, but its page sends students to municipal schemes: Horsens municipality guarantees housing for all international students before study start (Holstebro has a guarantee too) - under the site convention this counts as student housing through a public body.
- **UCN – University College of Northern Denmark (Danemarca)**, `dorms`: false → true. UCN has no campus housing but sends students to Studiebolig Aalborg, the city's joint student-housing portal, with Aalborg Municipality's housing guarantee - a public student-housing body under the site convention.
- **Dania Academy (Erhvervsakademi Dania) (Danemarca)**, `dorms`: false → true. Dania has no housing of its own, but its page points to Viborg Municipality's housing guarantee for students moving to the city (and Camp Logos opposite the campus) - a public route to student housing under the site convention.
- **University College Absalon (Danemarca)**, `dorms`: false → true. Absalon has no dorms of its own, but its page sends students to Kalundborgkollegiet and housing associations, and Kalundborg Municipality guarantees a bed at semester start - a public route to student housing under the site convention.
- **Business Academy Aarhus (Erhvervsakademi Aarhus) (Danemarca)**, `dorms`: false → true. The academy has no dormitories, but it sends students to Student Housing Aarhus, where Aarhus Municipality guarantees a student rental to new students starting in the autumn - a public student-housing body under the site convention (same body as for Aarhus University, which is true).
- **Universitat de Barcelona (Spania)**, `scholarships`: false → true. The ministry's grant page says EU citizens can get the general state grant; without residence or work in Spain they receive only the tuition amount. Same rule as on the other public-university sheets.
- **Universidad de Oviedo (Spania)**, `budget`: gratuit → mic. The free first-enrolment tuition is confirmed for EU citizens, but it is a yearly decision of the Asturian government with conditions (no previous degree, state grant requested if eligible, a minimum share of credits passed to keep it in later years, repeated subjects paid). A pupil reading the sheet will enrol in a later year, so the stable level is the public price, like the other Spanish public sheets.
- **Université Toulouse Capitole (Franța)**, `dorms`: false → true. By the site convention: the university's housing page sends students to the CROUS Occitanie residences (two are next to the campus) and does not say students must use the private market.
- **Université de Montpellier (Franța)**, `dorms`: false → true. By the site convention: for housing the university sends students to CROUS Montpellier (DSE) and does not say they must use the private market.
- **Politecnico di Milano (Italia)**, `budget`: mediu → mic. The fee is income-based (ISEE) with exemption for low incomes, the same structure as Politecnico di Torino and the other Italian public universities, all labelled 'mic'; the cost of living belongs in the note, not in the fee level.
- **Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) (Olanda)**, `dorms`: true → false. The housing page says TU/e provides limited housing support only for first-year international master's students; other students arrange accommodation independently.
- **Radboud University (Olanda)**, `dorms`: false → true. By the site convention: Radboud has an agreement with the student housing association SSH& and bachelor students who travel more than two hours can enter its room lottery with priority.
- **Wageningen University & Research (Olanda)**, `dorms`: false → true. By the site convention: the university sends students to Idealis, the student housing provider of Wageningen, where admitted students living far away get priority in summer.
- **Warsaw University of Technology (Politechnika Warszawska) (Polonia)**, `budget`: gratuit → mic. The B.Sc. offer page shows 'EU: no charge' only for Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Aerospace Engineering and Power Engineering; the other seven English-taught programmes (Architecture, Electric and Hybrid Vehicles, Environmental Engineering, Environmental Protection, Civil Engineering and both Computer Science programmes) list a fee in PLN for EU/EFTA citizens - the same mixed situation as the University of Warsaw sheet, which is 'mic'.
- **Imperial College London (Marea Britanie)**, `scholarships`: false → true. Imperial now runs the Imperial Inspires scholarships for international students paying the Overseas fee, including undergraduates in its four faculties.
- **University of Manchester (Marea Britanie)**, `scholarships`: true → false. The undergraduate merit scholarship for international students (Global Futures) is limited to residents of a list of countries that does not include Romania or any EU country; the Romania country page has no scholarships section.
- **Northumbria University (Marea Britanie)**, `scholarships`: true → false. No scholarship that a student living in Romania can get was found on the official pages (only sport scholarships for athletes are mentioned); 2027/28 awards were not yet published, so re-check.

## Fișe verificate doar parțial (site-ul oficial nu s-a deschis)

- **Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (Germania)** — www.hu-berlin.de answers every request with an Anubis bot-check page, which I did not bypass, so nothing on this sheet could be verified. A human should check in a browser especially: (1) the application route - the sheet says "direct la universitate sau prin uni-assist"; (2) "unele programe în engleză" and the "engleza" certificate for bachelors; (3) the generic tuition wording "sau taxe foarte mici" (the newer Berlin sheets say "fără taxă de școlarizare; doar o contribuție semestrială", which at TU and FU includes the transport ticket); (4) the dorm note, which does not say whose the halls are (studierendenWERK Berlin) or that a place is not guaranteed.
- **Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg (Germania)** — Tuition confirmed ("An der Universität Würzburg gibt es keine Studiengebühren"; semester contribution = Studierendenwerk fee + semester ticket). Ten faculties, VPD from uni-assist before applying, B2 at application for open programmes and DSH-2 at enrolment all confirmed. The university describes living costs in Würzburg as favourable compared with other German cities; the sheet says "mediu" (opinion, left as is).
- **Leibniz Universität Hannover (Germania)** — Confirmed: nine faculties, B1 at application / C1 at enrolment, VPD only for non-EU applicants, semester fee with the Germany-wide ticket, long-term fees only after the standard period plus six semesters, Deutschlandstipendium yearly for two semesters, open to admission applicants, social criteria considered. "Almost all bachelors start only in winter" was not re-checked in detail.
- **Tilburg University (Olanda)** — www.tilburguniversity.edu answered HTTP 403 to every page I tried (bachelor overview, application and admission, scholarships), so nothing on this sheet could be verified against the official site. Only the national rules (statutory fee for EU citizens) were confirmed elsewhere. A human should open the four recorded source pages in a browser.
